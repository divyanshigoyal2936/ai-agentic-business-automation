🚚 Delivery Policy
We process all orders within 1–2 business days and deliver them within 3–7 business days 
depending on the location. Orders above ₹999 qualify for free delivery, while orders below
₹999 may have a flat shipping charge of ₹79. Once your order is dispatched, tracking details
will be shared through email or SMS.

💰 Refund Policy
Customers can request a return or refund within 7 days of delivery for eligible products.
The item must be unused and in its original packaging. Once the returned product is inspected
and approved, the refund will be initiated within 2–3 business days to the original payment method.
Refunds may take an additional 5–7 business days to reflect in your account.

🛡️ Warranty Policy
Eligible products are covered by a 1-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects from
the date of delivery. The warranty does not cover accidental damage, improper use, water damage,
or unauthorized repairs. For warranty claims, customers must provide their order ID or purchase 
invoice and contact our support team for assistance
